# 2011–2012 — Post-Nobel acceleration

**Evidence:** Well-supported (program-level)

## Highlights

- CVD graphene on copper and transfer methods scale in labs.
- Flexible electronics, RF transistors, and photonics demos multiply.
- Policy discussions in the EU toward a large coordinated initiative (Flagship).

## Tone of the era

High expectations (“graphene will replace silicon”) mixed with sober engineering constraints (band gap, contact resistance, manufacturing uniformity). That tension still shapes industry communication today.
