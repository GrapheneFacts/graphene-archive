# 1962 — Boehm et al.: monolayer carbon from reduced graphite oxide

**Evidence:** Well-supported (with later historiographic discussion)  
**Topics:** research

## What happened

Hanns-Peter Boehm and colleagues report extremely thin carbon films from chemical reduction of graphite oxide, including identification of **monolayer** flakes (TEM / diffraction lineage).

Geim and Novoselov have publicly credited this line of work as an experimental precursor; the 2004 Manchester contribution is usually framed as *unambiguous isolation, identification on insulating substrates, and electronic measurement* that launched the modern field.

## Naming

The word **graphene** is associated with Boehm and co-authors in the mid-1980s (often cited as 1986) as a systematic name for single sheets within the graphite family.
