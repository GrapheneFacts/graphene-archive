# 2010 — Nobel Prize in Physics

**Evidence:** Well-supported  
**Topics:** research, policy (science policy visibility)

## What happened

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awards the **Nobel Prize in Physics** to Andre Geim and Konstantin Novoselov “for groundbreaking experiments regarding the two-dimensional material graphene.”

## Effect

- Massive public and funding visibility.
- Acceleration of national programs and industrial roadmaps.
- Graphene becomes shorthand for the wider 2D materials family that follows (hBN, TMDCs, etc.).
