# 2014–2019 — Roadmaps, pilots, and early products

**Evidence:** Well-supported at overview level

## Trends

- Production capacity for powders/platelets and CVD films rises (tonnage claims vary by quality grade — always check definitions).
- Early commercial niches: composites, coatings, additives, thermal interface materials, conductive inks, sports equipment demos.
- Standardization and ISO/IEC terminology efforts begin to matter as “graphene” products proliferate with uneven quality.
- Research shifts partly from pure graphene toward **van der Waals heterostructures** and other 2D materials.
