# 2013 — EU Graphene Flagship launches

**Evidence:** Well-supported  
**Topics:** policy, research, industry

## What happened

The European Commission launches the **Graphene Flagship** (with the Human Brain Project) as a FET Flagship: a long-horizon, billion-euro-class coordination effort to move graphene and related materials from labs toward applications and industry.

## Why it matters

One of the largest structured public bets on 2D materials, spanning academia and companies, standards, roadmaps, and pilot lines over the following decade (original core phase often described through ~2023, with follow-on coordination under Horizon Europe).
