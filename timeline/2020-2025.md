# 2020–2025 — Industrialization and the end of Flagship phase one

**Evidence:** Well-supported at overview level

## Trends

- Flagship-era projects report patents, spin-outs, and product counts (treat marketing tallies cautiously; prefer primary filings).
- Energy storage, composites, sensors, and EMI/thermal applications remain common industry targets.
- Occupational exposure and safe handling guidance strengthen (e.g. banding and workplace studies into the mid-2020s).
- Quality, dispersion, and “graphene-washed” products remain central market issues.
