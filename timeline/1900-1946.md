# 1900–1946

**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

## Highlights

- Early 20th century: structure of graphite refined by X-ray crystallography (layered honeycomb sheets stacked in 3D).
- 1918 (approx.): work on graphite oxide paper / oxidized graphite products continues the Brodie chemical lineage.
- Graphite remains an industrial material; “one atom thick free crystal” is not yet an experimental field.

## Bridge to theory

By the 1940s, understanding bulk graphite’s electronics motivates treating a **single layer** as a theoretical starting point — the step P. R. Wallace takes in 1947.
