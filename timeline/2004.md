# 2004 — Isolation and measurement at Manchester

**Evidence:** Well-supported  
**Topics:** research, engineering

## What happened

Andre Geim, Konstantin Novoselov, and collaborators isolate few-layer and monolayer graphene by mechanical cleavage from graphite (“Scotch tape” method), transfer flakes to oxidized silicon, and report electric-field effect in atomically thin carbon films.

**Landmark paper:** Novoselov et al., *Science* **306**, 666 (2004) — “Electric field effect in atomically thin carbon films.”

## Why this year is the inflection point

Not the first time monolayer carbon was ever produced chemically, but the combination of:

1. Clear monolayer identification,
2. Devices on an insulating substrate,
3. Electronic measurements that matched the emerging 2D-electron-gas / Dirac picture,

…triggered a sustained global research boom.

## Culture note

Often tied to informal “Friday night experiments” at Manchester — playful exploration alongside main projects.
