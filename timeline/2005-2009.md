# 2005–2009 — Dirac physics and a research gold rush

**Evidence:** Well-supported

## Science

- **2005:** Experiments establish graphene carriers as massless Dirac fermions (relativistic-like dispersion).
- Rapid growth in quantum Hall effect studies, Raman characterization standards, and CVD beginnings.
- Theory and experiment reinforce each other; citation explosion after 2004.

## Institutions and industry seeds

- National labs and universities stand up graphene programs.
- Early company and patent activity around nanoplatelets, transparent conductors, and composites.
- **2009:** Specialist industry media (e.g. Graphene-Info) and broader press coverage expand.
