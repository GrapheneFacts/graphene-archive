# 1963–2003 — Name, epitaxy, and near misses

**Evidence:** Well-supported historical consensus

## Naming

**1986 (commonly cited):** Boehm, Setton, Stumpp and colleagues help fix **graphene** as the term for single graphite sheets (graphite + -ene).

## Epitaxy and surface science (1970s onward)

Monolayer carbon is grown or observed on metal surfaces and on silicon carbide (thermal decomposition / sublimation routes). These are crucial materials-science threads but often coupled to substrates — distinct from freely handled monolayers on SiO₂ used in the 2004 transport experiments.

## Theory side notes

Discussions of 2D crystalline order (e.g. Mermin–Wagner context) shaped expectations about whether strictly 2D crystals could exist as free-standing materials — part of why experimental isolation was surprising to many.

## Patents and nanoplatelets (early 2000s)

Early 2000s filings (e.g. nano graphene platelet / single-layer production concepts) show industrial interest immediately before the 2004 *Science* paper.
