# Graphene timeline

History of ideas, theory, isolation, and the path to today’s research and industry — organized by era and year.

**Evidence style:** historical consensus from standard sources (Wallace 1947, Boehm, Geim & Novoselov 2004, Nobel 2010, EU Flagship, etc.). Contested credit is noted briefly. This is an archive orientation guide, not a peer-reviewed history monograph.

---

## Quick spine

| When | What |
|------|------|
| Ancient–1800s | Graphite known and used; chemistry of graphite oxide begins |
| **1859** | Brodie — lamellar structure of oxidized graphite |
| **1947** | Wallace — band structure of a single graphite layer |
| **1948** | Ruess & Vogt — early TEM of thin carbon films from GO |
| **1962** | Boehm et al. — monolayer flakes from reduced GO (later credited by Geim/Novoselov lineage) |
| **1986** | Term **graphene** formalized (Boehm et al.) |
| **2004** | Geim & Novoselov — isolation, identification, device measurements (*Science*) |
| **2005** | Dirac-fermion / relativistic carrier picture in experiment |
| **2010** | Nobel Prize in Physics |
| **2013** | EU Graphene Flagship launched (~€1bn class initiative) |
| **2010s–2020s** | Scale-up, composites, electronics R&D, standardization, commercial additives |
| **2026-W38** | [Current weekly compile](../weekly/2026-W38.md) |
