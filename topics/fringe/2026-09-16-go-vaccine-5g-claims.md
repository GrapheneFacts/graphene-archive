# GO in vaccines / 5G activation claims

**Date logged:** 2026-09-16  
**Window noted:** 2026-W38  
**Evidence:** Fringe  
**Topics:** fringe, health (context only)

## Summary

Recycled claims that COVID vaccines contain graphene oxide and/or that GO can be “activated” by 5G or emergency alerts. Mid-September 2026 posts repeated 2021-era microscopy and petition-hearing material.

## Why Fringe

- Licensed vaccine ingredient lists (FDA, EMA, MHRA and manufacturers) do not include graphene oxide.
- GO has been used in **lab imaging** (e.g. cryo-EM) of spike protein — that is not the same as a formulation ingredient.
- Viral “self-assembly” clips have been traced by fact-checkers to a pre-COVID high-voltage physics demonstration, not vaccines or cell networks.
