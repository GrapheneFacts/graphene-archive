# Penn State nanoscale wrinkles in graphene as electrical speed bumps

Penn State-led work shows that nanoscale wrinkles in graphene act as local electrical “speed bumps.” Sharp curvature produces measurable flexoelectric polarisation far stronger than larger bends. Published in Advanced Materials. https://Graphenefacts.org
