# COnovate DOE contract for eCOphite graphene-monoxide anode additive

## Text

COnovate received a 24-month U.S. DOE Critical Minerals and Materials Accelerator contract of $1.25 million to scale eCOphite, a graphene-monoxide anode additive. Company-stated goal: less mined graphite and clearer state-of-charge tracking in LFP cells.
Evidence: Well-supported as a funding award. Product performance remains company-stated.
