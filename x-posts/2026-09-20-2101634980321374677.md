Weekly record — 14–20 Sep 2026.
The item already moving on X: GMG reports BIC-tested 1 Ah graphene cells, 489 cycles at 10C with no measured fade. LTO on the same profile was near end-of-life by 64 cycles. Small cells. Energy density still being optimized.
2/ Applications / devices.
UCLA used a holed graphene sheet as a molecular stencil on gold for patterned nanoelectronics and possible biosensors (ACS Nano). Separate track: Stony Brook patented a GO film for dew point and icing detection — patent, not a licensed medical device.
3/ Finance / build-out.
DOE awarded COnovate $1.25M to scale a graphene-monoxide anode additive aimed at less mined graphite. GMG also reported a ministerial opening of its Gen 2.0 plant in Queensland — a company + government event, not an audit of tonnes shipped.
4/ Fringe this week was recurrence, not a new assay.
Posts again claimed graphene oxide in COVID shots and 5G activation. Public ingredient lists (FDA, EMA, MHRA) do not include GO. GO used in some lab imaging of spike protein is not an ingredient list.
